A little radar for seasonal coffee, specialty matcha, and the independent cafes making something worth leaving the house for. Starting in Minneapolis, St. Paul, and the surrounding metro.

An independent project, still taking shape. Coffee Radar is our working name.

01 / THE SOURCES

From the cafe itself.

We gather drink announcements from public Instagram posts and cafe menu pages. Automated tools help turn those posts into searchable listings. Open a drink to see its source and, when available, the original caption.

Prices appear only when a source states them. Drink photos come from cafe posts or menus; illustrated cards are labeled as drawings.

02 / THE FINE PRINT

Menus move fast.

A listing means we found a mention, not that we have checked today's stock. Cafes sell out, change recipes, and wrap up seasonal menus. Check with the cafe before a special trip, especially for ingredients or dietary needs.

We hide older listings and track last-call announcements. Human reviews help fill the gaps when a post doesn't tell the whole story.
